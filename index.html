About the Area Study Centre

Promoting advanced research and specialized academic understanding of the political, economic, social, and cultural dynamics of North America, Latin America, and Africa.

Legal Foundation

Established under the 1975 Area Study Centres Act No. XLV of the Government of Pakistan. An autonomous federal institution funded by the Ministry of Education through the Higher Education Commission (HEC).

QAU Affiliation

Academically integrated within Quaid-i-Azam University and formally affiliated with its Faculty of Social Sciences, providing rigorous graduate programs and multidisciplinary research advisory.

Core Objectives

To conduct specialized research on foreign policies, socio-economic issues, security frameworks, and regional developments across North America, Latin America, and African nations.

Academic Programs

Offering multidisciplinary postgraduate degrees focused on area studies, international relations, regional politics, and socio-cultural frameworks.

Graduate Program

M.Phil. in American Studies

A comprehensive 2-year program focusing on U.S. foreign policy, domestic politics, economic systems, literature, and contemporary international relations.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Eligibility: 16 Years of Education in Social Sciences/Humanities
  • Coursework & Research Thesis

Doctoral Program

Ph.D. in American & Area Studies

Advanced doctoral research program dedicated to in-depth analytical inquiry into North/South American and African geopolitical affairs, international security, and bilateral diplomacy.

  • Duration: 3 to 5 Years
  • Eligibility: M.Phil / MS Degree with 3.0+ CGPA
